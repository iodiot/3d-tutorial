Three.js from Scratch

3D for the web with Three.js, WebGL and WebGPU

A step-by-step series for frontend developers: from a first lit cube to shaders, WebGPU and compute. Every part comes with diagrams, screenshots and a live demo you can open in your browser.

    Geometry, Materials and Textures

    Models, Light and Shadows

    Oct 25, 2026 · Read · Live demo

  4. 04
    Your First Shaders

    Write your own GPU code with ShaderMaterial: vertex and fragment shaders, uniforms, varyings and UVs, animated vertex waves, gradients, noise and a fresnel rim, patching a lit material with onBeforeCompile, and how GLSL, WGSL and TSL fit together. With diagrams, screenshots and a live demo.

    Oct 26, 2026 · Read · Live demo

  5. 05
    WebGPU and TSL

    Switch Three.js to WebGPURenderer: async init and the automatic WebGL 2 fallback, node materials, and TSL, the JavaScript shading language that compiles to WGSL or GLSL. Port the Part 4 waves and fresnel rim to TSL, read the generated shaders, and move 40,000 particles on the GPU with a compute shader. With diagrams, screenshots and a live demo.

    Oct 27, 2026 · Read · Live demo

  6. 06
    Post-Processing and Bloom

    Render the scene to a texture first, then change the whole picture: render targets and ping-pong buffers, EffectComposer with RenderPass, UnrealBloomPass, OutputPass, a custom lens ShaderPass and FXAA/SMAA, why pass order and tone mapping matter, and the same chain on WebGPURenderer with RenderPipeline, pass() and TSL bloom, including selective bloom with MRT. With diagrams, screenshots and a live demo.

    Oct 28, 2026 · Read · Live demo

  7. 07
    Faster Frames

    Make three.js scenes fast, and know why they are: measuring a frame with renderer.info, stats.js and Chrome DevTools, CPU-bound vs GPU-bound, draw calls, merging geometries, InstancedMesh and BatchedMesh, LOD and frustum culling, texture, KTX2 and pixel-ratio budgets, the cost of shadows, dispose() and memory leaks, rendering on demand, and what changes on WebGPURenderer. With a live stress test, diagrams and screenshots.

    Nov 4, 2026 · Read · Live demo

  8. 08
    Interaction and Physics coming next

    Picking objects with Raycaster, including instances, dragging them, and a physics engine (Rapier) so thousands of instanced objects fall, collide and stack.
